FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Send us a WhatsApp and a real person replies, day or night.

Is the price with or without fuel?

Without fuel. The car comes with fuel noted on handover and goes back at the same level. On tours, we can add a fuel package at pump price if you prefer one bill.

Can I take a self-drive car out of the city?

Yes, within Punjab, KP and Islamabad with prior notice. Northern areas trips are with driver only, for safety and insurance.

What if the car breaks down?

Call or WhatsApp our 24/7 line. Within twin cities a replacement car reaches you within two hours; outstation we arrange the nearest one.

Do you decorate wedding cars?

Yes. Fresh-flower decor for the bridal car and matching ribbons for the convoy. Add it in the booking and share your colour theme.

How do I pay?

Cash, bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa or card on pickup. Corporate clients get a monthly invoice.

When do I get my self-drive deposit back?

Within 24 hours of return, after a quick check for damage, fines and extra kilometres.
