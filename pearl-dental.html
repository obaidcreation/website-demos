    FAQ

    Common questions

    Does root canal treatment hurt?

    No. The tooth is fully numbed first. Most patients say it feels like a normal filling.

    Do you treat children?

    Yes. Dr. Maham sees kids from age 3, with a calm and friendly first visit.

    Can I pay for braces in installments?

    Yes. Braces and aligners can be paid monthly over the treatment period.

    How are instruments cleaned?

    Every instrument goes through an autoclave and is sealed in a fresh pouch for each patient.
